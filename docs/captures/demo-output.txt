==============================================================================
  At threshold 0.85: 5/10 attacks served from the poisoned
  entry, with 0 filter invocations. The filter would have
  flagged all 10 of them at score 1.000 - it was simply never asked.

  The variants that get through are the CHEAPEST ones: casing,
  punctuation, a filler word. No paraphrase model required.

  That gap is what SentryGate closes - the verdict is computed once
  at screening and stored in the Qdrant payload, so a cache lookup
  is a verdict-filtered search and the filter IS the predicate.
